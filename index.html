Research

My final-year research work

Beyond Sentiment: Detecting Emotional Distress and Support-Seeking Behavior in Bangla Social Media Using Multi-Task Learning

Final Year Design Project I · Southeast University

Sentiment analysis can only label a post as positive, negative or neutral — but a post expressing emotional distress and a post asking for help can both look simply "negative". Our research goes beyond sentiment: we are building a multi-task learning model — one shared text encoder with task-specific classification heads — that detects emotional distress and support-seeking behaviour as two separate signals in Bangla, English and Banglish (code-mixed) social media posts. Our dataset is annotated with five label groups — sentiment, emotional distress, support-seeking, depression risk and emotion — and we compare BanglaBERT and XLM-RoBERTa using accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score.

Bangla NLP Multi-Task Learning BanglaBERT XLM-RoBERTa Sentiment Analysis

A research prototype for academic study — not a clinical or diagnostic tool.
